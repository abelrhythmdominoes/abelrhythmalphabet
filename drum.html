Compose your own groove

Tap the cells: 16 steps = one bar of sixteenth-notes (4 beats), repeated 4 times. Changing a preset copies it to “My groove”, saved on this device.

Tracks you don’t play sound as the backing. Your keys, pads and MIDI drums are the ones set up in ABEL Play (D snare, F kick, J hi-hat, H tom).
